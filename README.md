# Städtli Kebap

Static restaurant website. `dist/index.html` contains the content and `dist/style.css` the responsive styling.

## Replace the food photos

Replace `dist/assets/hero-kebap.png` and `dist/assets/durum.jpg` with the restaurant's photographs, keeping the filenames. The kebap image is used in the hero and the first food tile. Update alt text and remove symbol-image notices and stock-photo credits when using owned images.

## Content

Address, telephone and opening hours are based on the supplied business information. All ordering buttons link to https://www.stdtlikebap.ch/ . Food categories were checked against that ordering website on 15 September 2026. No individual product prices or delivery promises are made.
